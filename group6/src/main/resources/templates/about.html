About SkillTrail

An online learning platform built to make good courses simple to find, easy to follow and rewarding to finish.

Our story

Learning that fits your life

SkillTrail brings practical, expert-led courses in programming, data, design, business, languages and marketing to one place. Every course is split into short sections with lessons and quizzes, so you always know what to do next.

Study on your phone or laptop, pick up exactly where you stopped, and earn a certificate when you finish. We keep the platform simple so you can spend your time learning, not searching.

  • Short video, text and file lessons
  • Quizzes after every section with your best score saved
  • Downloadable certificates when you complete a course
